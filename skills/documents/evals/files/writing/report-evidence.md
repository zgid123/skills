# Synthetic library-pilot evidence

All names, records and counts in this fixture are invented solely for evaluation. They are not real publications or actual research findings. Cite the note IDs below as supplied internal notes; do not invent bibliographic metadata.

## N1: Visit log

The baseline week recorded 80 library visits, of which 20 included borrowing. The following pilot week recorded 100 visits, of which 35 included borrowing. The pilot combined a new shelf-sign system with a staffed recommendation desk. Different visitors and different scheduled events occurred across the two weeks. The log counts visits, not unique people; repeat visitors were not tracked. There was no random allocation or concurrent comparison group.

## N2: Voluntary exit survey

Twelve visitors responded in the baseline week; eight said they found the book they wanted. Fifteen responded in the pilot week; eleven gave that answer. The survey was voluntary, did not link to visit-log records and did not measure nonrespondents. No interviews or statistical tests were conducted.

## N3: Team planning note

The team is considering a four-week extension with visitor tracking that avoids storing names, a consistent survey question and a review of whether desk hours exclude evening visitors. These actions have not happened. Staff time and costs have not been measured. The instructor wants a reasoned recommendation about whether to extend the pilot, with conditions and limits, rather than a claim that it has already been proven effective.
