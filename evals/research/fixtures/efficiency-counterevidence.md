# Fictional S: inspected methods and results

Sen (2026), Community Participation Reports, non-peer-reviewed preprint. Supplied sections only; not a full paper.

Methods: A survey compared 300 adults who opted into reminders with 300 who did not at six centers. Attendance was self-reported for the previous year. No adjustment for confounding or causal identification is described in the extract.

Results: 180/300 opt-in participants reported attendance, versus 210/300 others. Authors speculate that reminders discourage attendance. The extract supplies no uncertainty interval.

The assignment, outcome ascertainment, and observation period differ from R. The reported association does not identify a causal effect.
